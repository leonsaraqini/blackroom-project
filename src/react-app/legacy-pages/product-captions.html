Stop typing captions by hand.

Kairos Captions is a panel that lives inside Premiere Pro. Mark an In/Out, press Transcribe, and every word of your edit lands in an editable transcript, all processed on your own machine. Fix what you need, pick a look, and Add captions puts an animated, transparent overlay on a new top track. No After Effects, no uploads.

Transcribe in one click

Kairos listens to every clip under your In/Out range and turns speech into word-timed captions in 100 languages. Pick the language or let it auto-detect. Everything runs locally, and an NVIDIA GPU makes it several times faster.

  • 100 languages + auto-detect
  • Isolate voice from music & noise
  • Vocabulary for names, brands & slang

Edit words like text

Every caption is a row of word chips. Double-click to fix a word, click to highlight it as a keyword, and split, merge or reorder captions. Words the model wasn't sure about are underlined in orange, so you know exactly where to look.

  • Fix, split, merge & reorder captions
  • Keyword highlights in one click
  • Edited words re-timed from the audio

Seven looks, live preview

Start from Hormozi, Beast, Karaoke, Boxed, Neon, Clean or Minimal, then make it yours. Change font, size, colors, stroke, background, shadow, spacing and animation. The live preview plays your real words as you tweak.

  • 7 presets, every setting editable
  • 5 entrance animations · 6 active-word highlights
  • Live preview with your own transcript

Straight onto your timeline

Add captions renders a transparent, animated ProRes 4444 overlay and places it on a fresh top track at your In point, cut at every caption. You also get an editable .srt file. If you change anything later, update the overlay without transcribing again.

  • Transparent overlay + .srt
  • New top track: nothing below is touched
  • Update anytime, no re-transcribing

Good to know

Which app is Kairos Captions for?

It's a panel for Adobe Premiere Pro 2021 (15.0) or newer on Windows. After installing, open it from Window ▸ Extensions ▸ Kairos Captions, mark an In/Out on your sequence and press Transcribe.

Is my audio uploaded?

No. Transcription runs entirely on your computer. You only need internet once to download the speech models, and briefly about once a day to check your license. If you're offline, it keeps working for up to 7 days.

Do I need After Effects?

No. Captions are rendered as a transparent overlay and land directly on your Premiere timeline.

How does billing work?

Kairos Captions is a simple [monthly] subscription. Plans start at $[price] / month, every plan starts with a 14-day free trial, and you can cancel anytime.

How is it delivered and installed?

You'll receive the installer by email. Run it, open Premiere, and the panel sets up its own engine. The speech models download on first use and resume if interrupted.
